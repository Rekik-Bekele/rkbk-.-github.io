# Principles of Genetics (AnSc 2012)
Second-year BSc, semester 2. Lecturer:  Rekik Bekele
## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this course, students will be able to:
1. Explain the fundamental principles of animal genetics and evaluate their applications in animal breeding, reproduction, and production systems.
2. Analyze the genetic principles governing cell division (mitosis and meiosis) and apply Mendelian inheritance concepts in animal reproduction and breeding practices.
3. Interpret the mechanisms of DNA replication, gene action, linkage, sex determination, crossing over, and gene mapping in relation to animal genetics.
4. Differentiate between chromosomal aberrations and gene mutations, and assess their effects on animal growth, reproduction, and productivity.
5. Evaluate the applications of genetic engineering and biotechnology in improving animal reproduction, production, and genetic resources.
